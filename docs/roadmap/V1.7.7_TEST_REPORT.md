### 6.1 Sprite updates in the vertical blank (No Signal, Maiya & Luna)

The games write sprite tables only in the vertical blank (`NG_VRAM_DEFER`);
what doesn't fit waits for the next one. `make ... PERF=2` builds count it
(`ng_perf`, read by symbol every frame by `tools/genre_qa.py`); each run is
an autopilot build on MAME's MVS driver.

| Build | Run | Frames with a sprite update left waiting | Longest run of such frames |
|---|---|---|---|
| No Signal, before the changes below | 240 s, Zoe | 2045 of 14204 (14%) | 51 |
| No Signal, fighters keep their sprite blocks, street of 21 strips placed one by one, five enemies at most | 240 s, Zoe | 1695 of 14204 (12%) | 32 |
| Maiya & Luna, Maiya's journey (before writing room set-ups at once) | 300 s | 989 of 17755 (5.6%); 429 of them in the first second after entering a room | 65 (the title and prologue) |

What waits, read from the SDK's write lists in the frames where something
did (No Signal, 200 s): fighters 4811 entries, props 835, sparks 499, the
rest under 25. A fighter's picture is 64 tiles, costed by the SDK at 13 of
the blank's lines, and the commit starts one only with half as much again
to spare: after the hero's picture, one more fits. No Signal now changes at
most one other fighter's picture a frame (the rest a frame or two later,
still moving), and places the street as two chains (two position writes a
frame instead of one per strip). Maiya & Luna writes a room's set-up at once
while the screen is dark. The measurements of those builds are added here
when run.

Sprites on one scanline in play were not read back (section 6 has the
title only). Counted from the layout: the street's chain took 32 sprites on
every line, 11 of them off the screen, and the hero and up to six enemies (seven
on EXPERT) eight each, so with props a crowd standing on the same lines could
reach the hardware's 96, past which the highest-numbered sprites on a line
(the nearest fighters') are not drawn. Now the street takes 21 and at most
five enemies are out (six on EXPERT).
