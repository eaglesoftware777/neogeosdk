Sky Lance Interceptor Artwork
============================

Source pack: Space Shooter Remastered by Kenney.
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication).

The demo imports these original PNG assets at a 32-pixel playfield size:
  enemyBlue1.png  -> zzzzzzzzzz_sky_enemy_a.png
  enemyRed2.png   -> zzzzzzzzzz_sky_enemy_b.png
  enemyGreen5.png -> zzzzzzzzzz_sky_enemy_c.png

Original alpha is retained. White hull highlights are not a transparency key.
The SDK's art conversion produces the Neo Geo tile and palette data.
