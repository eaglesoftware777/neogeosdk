No Signal Z Alpha - sound credits
==================================

The short voice lines in voices/ (the heroes' "bruh", "no cap", "you're
cooked" and "massive W", Rico's two lines and the announcer's "no signal")
are spoken by Piper with the en_US-libritts_r-medium voice, which is trained
on the LibriTTS-R corpus (Koizumi et al., 2023), licensed CC BY 4.0.

Every other effect and all the music are synthesized by tools/sound.py with
the SDK's instruments (sound/tools/ng_synth.py, ng_songs.py); the tunes are
original compositions written for this game.
