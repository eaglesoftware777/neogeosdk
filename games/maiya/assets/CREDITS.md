# Shadow Seven Artwork

`environments.png` holds the six original environment paintings the
missions are built from: the lantern district, the bamboo pass, the iron
harbour, the moonlit roofs (also the Crimson Keep), the furnace works and
the snow monastery.  Each becomes a far layer and a road strip.

The heroine, her allies, the rival clans and the guardians are the SDK's
own character set (`games/demo/artbox/in/characters`), scaled to the
playfield and recoloured by `tools/prepare_assets.py` into the clan
costumes; the eagle and the portraits come from the same set.  The ledge
beams, shuriken, knives, musket balls, fire, spikes, cage and pick-up icons
are drawn by the same script.

Audio is the NeoGeoSDK demo bank, used without modifying the driver or its
sample data.  Its source credits are in `games/demo/sound/SOURCES.md`.

This is an original adaptation of the ninja-action genre.  It contains no
commercial game data, disassembled code or assets from other games.  The
folder name is the working title.
