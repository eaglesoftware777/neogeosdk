SKY LANCE - OPERATION AURORA

STICK       Smooth flight on both axes. Hold left/right to bank to 45 degrees.
A           Primary fire. Hold for automatic fire; P raises weapon power.
B           Afterburner. It changes speed; it does not change fuel's countdown.
B + LEFT    Evasive left barrel roll (press B while holding left).
B + RIGHT   Evasive right barrel roll (press B while holding right).
B + UP      High-G climb (press B while holding up).
B + DOWN    Split-S dive (press B while holding down).
C           Pilot-specific super bomb. C:BMB and the bomb icon show stock.
D           Heavy missile. AMMO shows the remaining missiles.
B + D       Chaff. Can pull guided missiles onto a visible decoy cloud.

Maneuvers have a short cooldown. Normal stick movements use
gentle banks; the long hold reaches 45 degrees and releases smoothly to level.

ROOK / F-16   Silver-blue nimble fighter; precision bomb strike.
KIRA / F-35   Olive-green steady fighter; stealth missile barrage.
BLAZE / F-15  Charcoal-crimson fast heavy fighter; cluster bomb run.
These are arcade handling models with miniature aircraft proportions.

FUEL is seconds remaining in the mission: 240 seconds at launch, counting
through both flight and target battles. Zero fuel fails the mission. The F
pickup adds 30 seconds; coasting and losing a life do not refill the timer.

At each of three SAM/radar pads, the scenery stops. Defeat the defending
escorts and the mini boss, then destroy the exposed command target to resume
flight. All three targets earn the 2500-point objective bonus. Submarines do
not count. After the final waves the flight cruises to the route's end,
where the main boss waits in an open arena.

Avoid the marked radar circle to remain undetected. Entering it triggers six
extra defenders and doubles the site's missile rate. Chaff briefly spoofs
the radar and gives guided missiles a decoy to follow, but each release
rolls every missile: later routes, higher DIFFICULTY settings and bosses'
missiles fool it less often (a boss's hardly at all, except on EASY).
If one stays locked on, the radar scope turns red. Each mission starts
with six chaff releases; the cockpit's B+D counter shows the remaining stock.
Air and ground units have distinct projectiles. Super bomb damage grows by mission.

P power / M missiles / S shield / F fuel / T speed / bomb extra super
L spare life / star score chain / H hull repair.

AES and MVS launchers start fullscreen. Scanline launchers require MAME's
BGFX crt-geom shader. The cartridge packages are not yet verified on real boards.
